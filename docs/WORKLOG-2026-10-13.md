## Noise colour check (scripts/noise-psd.py) — most labels do NOT match the spectrum
- New tool `scripts/noise-psd.py` (numpy + ffmpeg). Welch PSD of the pre-whitened signal, both channels
  as power, 1/3-octave bands, power-law fit 40 Hz .. real upper limit (codec cut detected above 8 kHz).
  Analyses only the part the app plays (loopStart .. end - loopEndTrim from index.html).
  Colour bands = owner's definitions: white |a|<=0.3, peach .3-.7, pink .7-1.3, orange 1.3-1.7, brown 1.7-2.3, infrared >2.3.
- Calibrated on synthetic 1/f^a noise a = 0, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3 (wav and AAC 70k/32 kHz): error <= 0.05.
- Results: `docs/noise-check-2026-10-13/` (SVG per sound + results.json). Only `pink-soft-noise` (a 1.03)
  and `white-noise-radio` (a 0.00) match. All `brown-*` measure a 0.2-1.2 (pink/white), `orange-noise` a -0.23,
  `white-noise` a -0.73 (blue-ish), infrared-* a 1.1 / 1.5 / 1.8 / 4.5.
- The Play listing under review (all 7 languages) says every noise colour is verified by spectral analysis — owner to decide.
